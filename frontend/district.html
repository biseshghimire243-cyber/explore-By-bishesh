Popular Tourist Places

Phewa Lake & Barahi Temple

A tranquil freshwater lake offering scenic boat rides and views of the Annapurna mountain range.

Sarangkot Viewpoint

Famous for breathtaking sunrise and sunset views over the Himalayas, as well as paragliding spots.

World Peace Pagoda

A serene Buddhist monument situated on a hilltop overlooking Pokhara valley and Phewa Lake.

Famous Foods

🥟

Steamed & Fried Momos

Delicious Nepalese dumplings filled with spiced vegetables or minced meat, served with spicy tomato chutney.

🍛

Traditional Dal Bhat Tarkari

The staple Nepalese meal consisting of lentil soup, steamed rice, vegetable curry, and spicy pickles.

🍩

Sel Roti & Local Tea

Crispy, sweet ring-shaped rice bread traditionally prepared during festivals, best enjoyed with fresh milk tea.
